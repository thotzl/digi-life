- [[overview/_index|Overview]] — vision (whitepaper) and glossary
- [[architecture/_index|Architecture]] — runtime structure, engine, IPC, persistence, config, invariants, performance
- [[biology/_index|Biology]] — genome compiler, CTRNN brain, sensing, metabolism and species
- [[simulation/_index|Simulation]] — Ocean, Trainer, physics, analytics and balancing
- [[frontend/_index|Frontend]] — webview structure and rendering
- [[quality/_index|Quality]] — review findings (`RV-*`), testing, CI and tooling, runtime verification
- [[project-map/_index|Project Map]] — module-level code map
- [[research/_index|Research]] — Alife references and porting ideas (from `ideas.md`)
- [[decisions/_index|Decisions]] — ADRs (none yet) and template

Each topic has a `history` article documenting closed tickets.
